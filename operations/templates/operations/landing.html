FleetIQ

AI-Powered Fleet Operations and Maritime Intelligence Platform

FleetIQ is a full-stack maritime analytics platform for monitoring vessels, analysing telemetry data, generating operational alerts, and preparing machine-learning-based vessel risk predictions.

Open Dashboard → View Vessels

Operational Dashboard

KPI cards, alert charts, fleet health distribution, and interactive drill-down navigation.

Telemetry & Alerts

Simulated vessel telemetry powers automatic alerts for weather, fuel, speed, delay, and engine risks.

Fleet Health Scoring

Each vessel is classified as Healthy, Watch, or High Risk based on operational alert behaviour.

Machine Learning Pipeline

Feature engineering and Random Forest model training prepare FleetIQ for predictive vessel risk analytics.

Technology Stack

Django · Django REST Framework · PostgreSQL · pandas · scikit-learn · JavaScript · Chart.js · Railway
