Why FleetIQ?

From telemetry data to operational decisions.

Modern operations generate large volumes of telemetry, status updates, alerts, and operational events. However, raw data alone does not help decision-makers understand risk, performance, or operational health.

FleetIQ provides a centralized operational intelligence platform that transforms telemetry data into meaningful KPIs, operational alerts, fleet health metrics, and predictive analytics.

The platform combines Django, PostgreSQL, REST APIs, interactive dashboards, and machine learning workflows to provide visibility across an entire fleet from a single operational view.
